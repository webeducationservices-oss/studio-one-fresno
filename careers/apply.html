Two paths, one salon

Employee stylist

Structure, mentorship and a growth plan. You start hourly at California minimum wage ($16.90/hour), with commission once you hit your performance goals. Workdays run about 5.5 hours so you grow without burning out.

Booth rental

Run your own business in a luxury space. Full-time chair (6 days a week, 24/7 access) or a boutique flex chair (3 assigned days). See chair pricing

Shadowing program

Still in school or newly licensed? Spend time behind the scenes first. How shadowing works

What we look for

Our core values: growth, team player, consistency, innovation, self awareness and accountability. A positive, drama-free attitude matters as much as your color work.
