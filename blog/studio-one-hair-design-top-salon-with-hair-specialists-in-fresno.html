Specialized Services for Your Unique Hair Needs

At Studio One, we pride ourselves on offering a comprehensive range of specialized services to address all your hair needs. From luxurious extensions to low-maintenance color techniques and transformative keratin treatments, our specialists are dedicated to providing you with the highest quality care and achieving stunning results.

Extensions Specialists

Dreaming of longer, fuller hair? Our extension specialists are experts in various application methods, including our no-sew, and the innovative NBR extensions technique (check out more about NBR here: https://www.studioonefresno.com/nbr-extensions). We'll help you choose the perfect extension type and color match to seamlessly blend with your natural hair, creating a flawless and natural-looking result.

Low Maintenance Color

Tired of spending hours in the salon every few weeks? Our color specialists are skilled in creating beautiful, low-maintenance color techniques that will keep you looking fabulous between appointments. From balayage and ombre to root shadowing and foilayage, we'll create a customized color that complements your skin tone and lifestyle, minimizing the need for frequent touch-ups. We also use high-quality, nourishing color products to protect your hair and keep it looking healthy and vibrant.

Curly Hair and Keratin Treatments

Curly hair requires specialized care and attention. Our stylists are trained in cutting and styling curly hair to enhance its natural texture and volume. We also offer customized keratin treatments to smooth frizz, reduce curl, and improve manageability, leaving your hair silky, shiny, and healthy. Whether you want to embrace your natural curls or achieve a sleek and smooth look, our specialists will help you find the perfect solution for your hair.
