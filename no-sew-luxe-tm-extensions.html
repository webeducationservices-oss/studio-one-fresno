What to Expect

Details

  • No braiding, no sewing — installs in about 15 minutes per row
  • Invisible, weightless feel
  • Great intro option or transitional method
  • Maintenance depends on wear and lifestyle (every 4–6 weeks is ideal)

Keeping It Fresh

Move-Up Options

Mini Move-Up Around 4 Weeks

A quick adjust-and-lift without full removal.

Policy: Only one mini move-up is allowed between full move-ups to maintain scalp and hair health.

Full Move-Up Every 6–8 Weeks

Removal and reinstall depending on your hair texture, density, and scalp health.

Policy: A full reset keeps your install comfortable and your natural hair healthy between mini move-ups.
