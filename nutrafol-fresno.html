The Science

Nutrafol Targets 6 Root Causes of Thinning

Most hair supplements focus on a single nutrient. Nutrafol takes a whole-body approach to the factors that affect how your hair grows.

Hormones

DHT sensitivity, which can be influenced by genetics, stress, diet and toxins.

Stress

Physical or emotional stress, from a demanding job to having a baby.

Nutrition

Nutrient gaps from your diet or a compromised gut microbiome.

Lifestyle

Your surroundings, the products you use and the foods you eat.

Metabolism

How well your hair follicles receive the nutrients they need.

Aging

Declining collagen and elastin that weaken the scalp’s grip on each strand.

Depending on the formula, key ingredients include Sensoril® Ashwagandha, curcumin, saw palmetto, marine collagen peptides and a tocotrienol complex. Nutrafol’s formulas are free of gluten, dairy, soy, hormones, drugs and artificial colors.
