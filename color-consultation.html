Policies

You and your appointment are very important to me. I hold your appointment just for you, and I ask that if you must cancel or reschedule you kindly provide 48 hours’ notice. This way I can adjust my schedule and accommodate other clients.

If you miss your appointment without giving 48 hours’ notice, you will be billed a 50% service charge per service booked. These fees are expected to be paid prior to any future appointments.

For your convenience I provide a confirmation call 48 to 72 hours before your appointment. Please keep me informed of any changes to your contact information.

I strive to offer my guests the highest level of satisfaction. If you’re having challenges with your cut, color or extensions, let me know within seven days of your visit and I’m happy to correct the issue. Retail purchases may be exchanged at full credit within 14 days of purchase.
