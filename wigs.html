Your Custom Wig Journey

A Simple, Supportive, Transparent Process

We understand that choosing a custom wig is a deeply personal decision. Our goal is to make the process simple, supportive, and transparent from start to finish.

1

Step 1

Request a Consultation

Complete our Wig Consultation Form and tell us a little about your hair journey, goals, and what you’re hoping to achieve.

2

Step 2

Complimentary Phone Consultation

We’ll schedule a phone consultation to get to know you, discuss your concerns, answer your initial questions, and determine whether a custom wig is the right solution for your needs.

During this conversation we’ll discuss:

  • Your hair goals and lifestyle
  • The custom wig process
  • Estimated pricing and timeline
  • Insurance questions, if applicable
  • Whether a custom wig is the best option for you

If we feel we’re a great fit, we’ll schedule your Custom Wig Design Appointment.

3

Step 3

Custom Wig Design Appointment

This is where your custom wig truly begins.

During your appointment we will:

  • Review your personalized wig design
  • Discuss realistic fit and styling expectations
  • Take precise measurements
  • Select your color, density, length, and texture
  • Review any manufacturer recommendations specific to your custom piece
  • Answer any remaining questions before placing your order

Once you’re confident moving forward, your custom wig order will be finalized.

Because every wig is custom-made specifically for you, full payment is required before your order is submitted to the manufacturer. All custom wig orders are non-refundable once production begins.

We understand this is a significant investment. Financing and payment plan options are available for qualified guests. We’d be happy to discuss those options during your appointment.

4

Step 4

Your Custom Wig Is Crafted

Once your order has been placed, your wig is handcrafted specifically for you. Production times vary depending on the level of customization, and we’ll keep you updated throughout the process.

5

Step 5

Delivery & Personal Fitting

When your custom wig arrives, we’ll schedule your fitting appointment.

During this visit we’ll:

  • Ensure your wig fits comfortably and securely
  • Make any necessary adjustments
  • Teach you how to wear and care for your wig
  • Review styling techniques and maintenance
  • Answer any final questions so you leave feeling confident

We’re With You Every Step of the Way

Hair loss is a personal journey, and you don’t have to navigate it alone. Our goal is to create a custom solution that looks natural, feels comfortable, and helps you feel like yourself again. From your first phone call through your final fitting, we’re here to guide and support you every step of the way.
